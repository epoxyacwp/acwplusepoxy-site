Our Process

A great floor starts before the coating goes down.

We do not treat surface preparation like a hidden step. It is one of the most important parts of the system.

1

Inspect

We evaluate the concrete, existing coatings, cracks, moisture concerns and project access.

2

Prepare

The slab is mechanically prepared and repaired as required for the selected coating system.

3

Coat

Basecoat, decorative broadcast and protective topcoat are installed to the agreed system.

4

Walk Through

We review the finished floor, cure guidance and care instructions before the project is closed out.
